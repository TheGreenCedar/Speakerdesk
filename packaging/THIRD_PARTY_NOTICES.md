Speakerdesk uses local NVIDIA speaker diarization and Cohere transcription models through Rust and Apple MLX, plus Core ML for saved-voice embeddings. The Mac app contains no Python interpreter or Python application runtime. It is not affiliated with NVIDIA, Cohere, Apple, Tauri or the MLX conversion authors.
