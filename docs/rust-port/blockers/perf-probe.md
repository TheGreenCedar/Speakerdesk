# Historical Linux performance-probe limitation

The foundation ran on `Linux 6.12.94+ x86_64` with `rustc 1.99.0
(b940084d7 2026-09-28)`. The planned Silero, Sortformer and Cohere comparison
against the packaged Speakerdesk 0.6.6 Mac app could not run on that host.

This foundation-era observation is superseded by the real Mac application
measurements in [the implementation record](../implementation.md). Those
retained fixture measurements, their output differences and the explicitly
accepted extra Stop time determine local qualification. The placeholder probe
does not provide acoustic or product acceptance.
