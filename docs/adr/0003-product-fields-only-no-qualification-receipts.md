# Product fields only; integrity verified at download and load boundaries

0.7 stores only fields that the product reads: the frontend, exports, edit and merge rules, refinement reconciliation, voice eligibility and recovery. It drops admission, execution, source-hash and GPU qualification receipts and the per-packet evidence journals. Model artifacts are verified once, at download and when they are loaded, and the result is cached in a marker keyed by file identity. They are not re-proven on every request. The stopped migration showed the cost of the alternative: recursive receipt checks ran before any model executed, took minutes, and expired the product deadline. A future reader may expect receipts to come back for auditability. They should come back only as offline test evidence, never in the product path.
