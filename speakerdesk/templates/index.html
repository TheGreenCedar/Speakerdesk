Settings

Appearance

Meeting language

Automatic follows language changes during a meeting. Choose a fixed language if automatic detection isn’t installed or keeps guessing wrong. This default applies to new meetings and imports; change the current meeting from its header.

Local models

Download once to transcribe meetings offline. Audio never leaves your Mac.

First setup needs an internet connection and about 2.4 GB of free disk space while models unpack.

Recording access

Speakerdesk needs two macOS permissions to record meetings. Your Mac asks the first time you start; if you declined, turn them on here:

  1. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. Under Microphone, turn on Speakerdesk.
  3. Under Screen & System Audio Recording, turn on Speakerdesk to capture voices from your meeting app.

Tip: use headphones so Mac audio doesn’t leak into your microphone.

Voice recognition

Saved voices are recognized once when a new speaker appears. You can correct any name.

To save a voice, name a speaker from the speaker list above a transcript, then choose Remember voice. Uncertain speakers keep their current label.
